### English

- **Codex query performance**: new `CodexAppServerRPCClient` streams the Codex CLI app-server RPC stdout and returns as soon as both responses arrive, with a single 20 s deadline, immediate cancellation, and background child reaping. - **Analytics scan performance**: `UsageAnalyticsRepository` no longer polls with `NSLock.try()` + 10 ms sleeps; scans queue on a suspending, cancellable `SerialOfficialFetchGate`, and short locks only guard cache entries.

While `Resources/update_signing_public_key.txt` is empty, verification is off; once a public key and the `UPDATE_SIGNING_PRIVATE_KEY` secret are provisioned, the release pipeline requires signatures and uploads the `.sig`. Provisioning steps are in `RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md` §5; not enabled in this release.
- **Bundle size**: the 739 KB `app_icon_source.png` moved out of the app bundle into `scripts/assets/`; packaging still uses it to generate the icns.
